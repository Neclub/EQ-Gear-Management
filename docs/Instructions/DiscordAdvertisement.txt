# ⚔️ EQ Gear Management (EQGM)
### One report. One character or your entire box team. No more guessing.

EverQuest Live can output a snapshot of a character to a text file.
**EQGM** reads those files for everyone on your team and turns them into **one** Excel workbook or web report.

> **Mission:** Stop farming in the dark.
> See who is geared, who is behind, and what to farm next — before raid night, not after.

**What you can answer in minutes:**
> 🛡️ Who is wearing what — color-coded by how current it is
> 📉 Whose slots are still last expansion
> 📜 Who still needs spell runes
> 🏆 Who is missing collections, quests, or raid achievements
> 💎 Which augs to wear, move, or farm
> 👑 What Raid BiS looks like *this* expansion

No extra installs. Download it, double-click it, point it at your EverQuest folder.
**These are suggestions based on the stats your class uses. Use the numbers, then decide whether a change is worth it.**
